Read this before you trust a number

Aid per athlete is for the whole department, not your sport. Colleges report athletic aid as one total for all men's teams and one for all women's teams. Nothing says how much went to your sport. At a college with a football team, most of the men's money goes to football. Use the figure to see how well a department funds its athletes overall, not as an offer you might get.

Team spend per athlete is for the sport you picked. It covers the cost of running the team for each athlete: travel, hotels, meals, kit, equipment and officials. It leaves out scholarships and coach pay. It is the best sign in this data of how seriously a college takes that team.

NCAA Division III cannot give athletic scholarships at all. Those colleges show "No athletic aid". They can still give academic or need-based money, which this data does not cover.

Squad size is last season's count of athletes, and Division I roster rules changed in 2025. Read it as how big the team is, not as how many places are open.

Source: US Department of Education, Equity in Athletics Disclosure Act, 2024–25 collection. Every college that takes federal student aid and runs a sports programme has to file it. Nothing here is an offer, a prediction or advice. It is a map of where the teams and the money are, so you know which coaches are worth emailing.
