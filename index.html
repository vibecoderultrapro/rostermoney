School Division

Read this before you trust a number

Aid per athlete is school-wide, not sport-specific. The federal filing reports athletic aid as one total for all men's teams and one for all women's teams. It does not say how much went to your sport. At a school with a football team, most of that money is going to football. Treat the figure as a signal of how much a department spends on its athletes overall, not as an offer you might receive.

Division III cannot award athletic scholarships at all. Those schools appear here with no aid figure. They may still offer academic or need-based money, which this data does not cover.

Roster spots are last season's participant counts, and Division I roster rules changed recently. Read the number as squad size, not as openings.

Source: US Department of Education, Equity in Athletics Disclosure Act, 2024–25 collection. Every institution that receives federal student aid and runs an intercollegiate programme has to file. Nothing here is an offer, a prediction, or advice. It is a map of where the money and the roster spots are, so you know which coaches are worth emailing.
